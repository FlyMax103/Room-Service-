Good to Know · Useful Tips
Tip 01
No insects
Do not leave any food waste on the kitchen counter. This prevents ants and similar insects.
Tip 02
Keep doors closed
Do not leave doors open unattended to avoid cockroaches flying into the apartment.
Tip 03
Red-dot switch
Next to the main entrance on the left. The switch with the red dot turns off all electronics except the fridge. Use it when leaving.
